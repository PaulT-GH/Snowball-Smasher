About Snowball Smasher

A free tool for planning your way out of debt.

What it is

Snowball Smasher is a free debt payoff calculator. You enter your credit cards and loans, choose a strategy, and it shows a month-by-month plan, the date you could be debt-free, and the interest you'll pay.

Who makes it

Snowball Smasher is created and run by Paul Thomas in the United Kingdom.

What it does

  • Compares the debt snowball (smallest balance first) and the debt avalanche (highest interest first).
  • Keeps your total monthly payment steady, so money freed up by falling or cleared debts rolls onto the next debt.
  • Handles credit cards repaid as a percentage of the balance or a fixed amount, and loans with a fixed payment and term.
  • Lets you add a monthly extra amount, an annual bonus and one-off lump sums.
  • Exports your plan as a PDF or CSV file.

Your data

The calculator runs in your browser. Your figures are not sent to us. If you choose Save, they are stored only on your own device. Read our privacy policy for details.

Important

Snowball Smasher gives estimates, not financial advice. Please read our disclaimer. If you're struggling with debt, free help is listed on our resources page.
