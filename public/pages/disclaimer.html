Snowball Smasher is not financial advice. It is a calculator that gives estimates.

Estimates only

Results depend on the information you enter and on simplifying assumptions. For example, the calculator divides each APR by 12 to get a monthly interest rate and applies payments once a month. Real lenders may calculate interest differently (for example daily), apply fees, change rates, or handle payment dates in other ways. Your actual results will therefore differ from those shown.

Not advice, not regulated

The site does not provide regulated financial advice and is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. It does not consider your personal circumstances. Before making decisions about debt, consider speaking to a qualified adviser.

If you're struggling

Free, impartial help is available in the UK. See our list of free debt help services.

No guarantee

We make no promise that following any plan produced by the site will have a particular result. Always keep up the required payments on all your debts, and check the terms of your own agreements.
