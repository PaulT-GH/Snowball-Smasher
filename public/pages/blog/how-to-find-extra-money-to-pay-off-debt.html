How to find extra money to pay off debt faster

Practical ways to free up money for your debt payoff plan: review spending, cancel what you don't use, and put windfalls to work.

By Paul Thomas · 26 October 2026

The snowball works with whatever you put into it, but extra money speeds it up a great deal. Here are realistic places to look.

Found some extra money? Add it to the calculator as an extra monthly payment, a bonus or a lump sum.

1. Start with your real spending

Go through the last two or three months of bank and card statements and sort every payment into groups. Most people find a few payments they had forgotten about. You can't plan with numbers you haven't seen.

2. Cancel what you don't use

Subscriptions, memberships and add-ons are easy to forget. Cancel anything you haven't used recently. You can always sign up again later.

3. Re-shop your regular bills

Insurance, broadband, mobile and energy contracts often get more expensive when a deal ends. Compare prices when yours is due for renewal and see if a better deal is available.

4. Put windfalls to work

Bonuses, tax refunds and gifts can make a large dent in a debt. The calculator lets you add an annual bonus and one-off lump sums, with a month and year, so you can see the effect before you commit.

5. Sell what you no longer need

Clothes, electronics, furniture and tools can raise a useful lump sum. One sale won't change your life, but it can clear a small debt, which is a good start for a snowball.

6. Look at income as well as costs

A few extra hours of work, a small side project, or asking about a pay review can do more than cutting spending ever will. Even a small regular amount helps because it stays in the plan every month.

Keep a small buffer

Try to keep a little cash aside for emergencies. Without it, a surprise bill can push you back onto a card. Even a small cushion helps you stay on track.

Add your extra amount to the calculator and see how much sooner you could be debt-free.
