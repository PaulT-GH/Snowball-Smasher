When to get free debt advice (UK)

A payoff plan works when you can afford the payments. If you can't, free, impartial UK debt advice is available. Here's where to find it.

By Paul Thomas · 26 October 2026

A snowball or avalanche plan assumes you can afford at least the required payments on every debt. If you can't, or you are worried about bills, a calculator is not the answer. Free advice is.

If you can afford the payments, the calculator can help you plan a way forward.

Signs you should talk to someone

  • You're missing payments or borrowing to cover other debts.
  • You're getting letters or calls from lenders or debt collectors.
  • You're choosing between paying debts and paying for essentials such as rent, food or energy.
  • You feel anxious about opening post or looking at your accounts.

Free, impartial help in the UK

These organisations don't charge for advice:

  • MoneyHelper, the government-backed money guidance service.
  • StepChange, a debt charity.
  • National Debtline, free advice by phone and online.
  • Citizens Advice, free advice on debt, benefits and more.

Be wary of companies that charge fees to "sort out" your debts or that promise to wipe them. Free options exist, so you should never need to pay for basic debt advice.

What advisers can do

They can help you work out a budget, talk to your lenders, and explain the options available to you. Which of these is right depends on your circumstances, and that is exactly what they will help you work out.

Then come back to your plan

Once your finances are on a steadier footing, a clear payoff plan is a good way to move forward. The calculator will still be here.

Snowball Smasher is a calculator, not an advice service. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
