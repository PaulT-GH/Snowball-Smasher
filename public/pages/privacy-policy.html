Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use snowballsmasher.freeyourinnerself.com ("the site"). The site is run by Paul Thomas in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller. Contact: paul.thomas@gmail.com.

The calculator

The calculator runs in your web browser. The figures you enter (balances, rates, payments and so on) are not sent to our servers. If you press Save Inputs, they are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. You can remove them with Reset All or by clearing your browser's site data. When you export a PDF or CSV, the file is created on your device.

Information collected automatically

Our web host keeps standard server logs, which may include your IP address, the pages you request, and your browser type. These are used for security and to keep the site running.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts is used to load the fonts. Your browser requests them from Google, which can see your IP address.
  • Google AdSense may be used to show adverts. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Where required, we ask for your consent first. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com, and read how Google uses data in its partner sites information.

Cookies

The site itself does not set cookies. Advertising providers may, as described above. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion. Because we hold very little personal data, the easiest way to use them is to email us. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
