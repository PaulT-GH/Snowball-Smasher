Staying motivated

  • Write down your debt-free date from the calculator and put it where you will see it.
  • Celebrate each debt you clear. A small, cheap treat is fine.
  • Update your balances each month and re-run the calculator so you can see the progress.
  • Tell someone you trust about your plan. It's easier with support.
