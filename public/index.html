What is a debt snowball?

A debt snowball is a way of paying off several debts. You keep your total monthly payment the same, pay the required amount on every debt, and send everything left over to one target debt. When that debt is cleared, its payment rolls onto the next one, so your repayments get faster over time, like a snowball gathering speed.

Which debt is the target depends on the method you choose. The debt snowball targets the smallest balance first, for quick wins. The debt avalanche targets the highest interest rate first, which usually costs the least overall. Read our guide to choosing between them.
